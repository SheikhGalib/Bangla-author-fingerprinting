Why show both paradigms?

Important Honesty & Domain Limitations (§7 Contract)
Closed-Set Attribution

The model selects exclusively among the 5 trained authors. Given text by any other author or modern Bengali, it will name one of the five; there is no "abstain" option.

Historical Literary Domain

Trained on public-domain Bengali prose from 1820–1951. Modern Bangla, journalism, social-media posts, and rhymed verse fall out of domain.

Passage Length Sensitivity

Stylometric estimators require sample mass. Accuracy drops from ~83% at 200 tokens down to ~55% at 40 tokens. Passages below 80 tokens carry a prominent warning.

Empirical Accuracy (~83%)

Evaluated on work-disjoint splits to prevent character-name topic leaks. Roughly 1 in 6 predictions is wrong. Evidence is presented to assist human evaluation.
