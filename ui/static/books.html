How to read this page

Nine books, three per author. For each book you get an overall book summary, a breakdown of its stories and narrative arc, and a chapter-by-chapter directory, followed by stylometric measurements computed directly from the text.

Recurring names

Words that are frequent in this book, rare in the other eight, and spread across at least three chapters. Bangla has no capital letters, so names are found by behaviour, not spelling.

Distinctive words

Highest TF-IDF against the other eight books: the vocabulary that sets this book apart, rather than just its commonest words.

Style numbers

A few of the 29 structural measurements the SVM uses. Sentence length and dialogue rate alone separate the three authors.

Computing analytics from the corpus…

Notes describe the form of each book; all statistics are recomputed from corpus/ every time this page loads.
